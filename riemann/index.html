About this map

Each dot is a Lean file and each thread is an import. The white point at the top is the theorem. A file's height is the longest chain of imports from the theorem down to it. This is Mirza's map method; sideways position is a layout choice.

Seven stages: the submission by Levent Alpöge with Claude (Nov 20); four stages of cleanup by the Lean community (repo); and the readable and compact editions by Luke Sernau and colleagues at Anthropic, with Claude (repo).

Every number is counted from the Lean source of each stage. Files are packaging: on Nov 23 small files were merged into others and lines rose as Mathlib imports were spelled out. Code lines (no comments, blanks or imports) are the like-for-like measure.

Machine-checked; not yet read through by a mathematician. Watch the video.
